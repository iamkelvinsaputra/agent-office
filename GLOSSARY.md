# Agent Office

A tiny 3D world in the browser that shows live Claude Code sessions as toy figures, in an office or a fantasy theme.

## Fantasy theme

**Party**:
The fantasy-theme figures, one per session, each a warrior, mage, archer or priest.
_Avoid_: Heroes, team

**Foe**:
The single opponent the Party fights in the fantasy theme. The red dragon is one Foe; others (such as an earth golem) are interchangeable with it.
_Avoid_: Monster, boss, creature, enemy

**Big attack**:
A Foe's occasional strike back at the Party: the dragon's fire-breath sweep, the golem's ground slam.
_Avoid_: Special, ultimate

**Taunt**:
A Foe's show of strength between Big attacks and on waking: the dragon roars, the golem pounds its chest.
_Avoid_: Roar (that is the dragon's Taunt, not the general term)

**Flinch**:
A Party member leaning back, arms up, when a Foe's Big attack reaches them.
_Avoid_: Knockback, damage

**Asleep**:
The Foe's resting state while no Party member is working.
_Avoid_: Idle, dormant

**Hit reaction**:
How a Foe visibly shows it has been struck by the Party. It is always physical movement, never a change in brightness or colour.
_Avoid_: Flash, blink
